# Misery & Co. — English Garden Edition

Open `4-LATEST-Start-Game.html` in desktop Chrome. It contains the game code, styles and artwork; the camera model/engine still need a network connection. Camera video is processed locally.

## Play one day

1. Read the customer's request at the counter. Use the flask icon to go to the workbench.
2. Open the cabinet, choose ingredients and return to the workbench. Each recipe uses one of each required ingredient.
3. Select a tray ingredient and use **+**, or drag it into the kettle. Add two or three ingredients.
4. Light the burner. When warm, hold the stir control. Then hold the pour control, or drag the kettle toward the receiving bottle.
5. A finished product appears automatically after pouring. The green return control goes to the counter. Click the finished product to deliver it.
6. The rain-bottle sale control sells a traditional bottle for four coins, up to three per day.
7. Visit Town Hall to accept the daily task: sell three items, including one custom delivery. Claim its reward, close the shop for settlement, then start the next day.

Recipes: **Evening Bellflower + Collected Rainwater → Blooming Shower**; **Spotted Mushroom + Raven Feather → Moonlit Rest**; **Lantern Berries + Rust Maple Leaf + Collected Rainwater → Butterfly Lantern**. Incorrect combinations consume their ingredients and produce green sludge.

## Orchard and garden

In town, select the orchard. Click a resource and the girl walks toward it. Snip three flowers, pick and deposit ripe berries, or brush leaves aside to reveal mushrooms. Feathers and fallen leaves are gathered on arrival. Click the orchard sky to catch rain in a bottle. Resources replenish on the next game day.

The **✿** entry in town, orchard or cabinet opens the six-bed garden. Enable the camera and let one complete hand enter its view. Point at a bed and pause to select it; pinch and release to sow, open your palm to water, lift to accelerate growth, then pinch and lift to harvest. Relax your hand before switching beds. Each harvest adds three bellflowers to the shared cabinet. Stock above the cabinet cap is retained. Watered plants also grow while the garden page is running; there is no growth while away.

**Watch demo** shows the gestures without changing real inventory. Use the back control to save the garden and stop the camera. Game progress is browser-local; keep the same browser and file/origin to resume.
